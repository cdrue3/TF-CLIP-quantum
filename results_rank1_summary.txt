KEY FINDINGS
-------------------------------------------------
- ep30 LR drop causes collapse in all models (quantum and classical)
- Ring entanglement most robust to collapse (43.1% vs ~13% for others)
- Quantum models converge faster but plateau earlier than classical
- 768→8 compression bottleneck is likely cause of early plateau
- GPU fix (removing CPU-pinning) gives ~3x speedup on default.qubit
